# Pokemon-Blue-Legend-English
- The entire game has been translated into English, though some parts may still be in Spanish, or perhaps only the beginning of the game has been translated. While I haven’t played through the whole game yet, I will continue to explore it and fix any issues as I play and work on the translation.
- I’ve added some useful elements to the game to make it look better, such as main menu, summary screen, party menu,....
- In the future, I might translate it into Vietnamese to make the gaming experience easier for myself and other Vietnamese users.
FEATURES
- Explore Kanto, Johto, Hoenn regions.
- You can choose May or Brendan as the main trainer.
- All the maps are fully redesigned and renovated with customized graphics.
- Many new events and adventures.
- There many characters who are unknown and known also.
- Day/Night System.
- New and renewed mini-sprites.
- All Pokemon from the Gen I to Gen IV (maybe some of them will come from the Gen V also).
- The soundtracks are new.
